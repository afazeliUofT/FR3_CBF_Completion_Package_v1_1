# Scientific stopping decision

Candidate v4.5 is frozen after the complete companion-aware fixed-association scheduling test.
The added companion modes closed only 4 of the 523 v4.4 residual intervals. The remaining 519 intervals are therefore not a minor mode-library omission. They are a fixed-association serviceability limitation under the frozen q/RZF/power/EESS action model.

The paper will not claim a universal zero-floor guarantee. It will claim:

- hard long- and short-term EESS safety;
- statistically significant proportional-fair utility improvement;
- floor satisfaction whenever the bounded distributed action set is feasible;
- explicit detection and reporting of infeasible service intervals;
- large reduction in floor-violation burden relative to predictive and safe-static baselines.

This is scientifically preferable to another post-hoc scheduling variant. User reassignment and preregistered serviceability-aware admission remain explicit fallbacks/future work, not hidden corrections.
