# Claim boundaries

## Permitted after the packaged local gates pass

- exact algebraic floor/EESS formulation at fixed modes and fixed beams;
- deterministic unit, randomized, and original-export regression results;
- source-bound reproduction of the packaged seed-43999 material failure;
- verification that the original validated 128-port topology obeys the `q=0`
  power envelope for both 65/3 dB mode orientations;
- a candidate repair design ready for the preserved-channel CPU diagnostic.

## Not permitted before returned Rorqual evidence is independently reviewed

- stating that seed 43999 has been repaired;
- stating that the bounded local action class is feasible or infeasible;
- treating seed 43999 as confirmatory evidence;
- authorizing seeds 44000--44029;
- claiming calibration, regulatory compliance, or population-level performance;
- claiming that fixed-beam infeasibility proves infeasibility under beam
  recomputation, reassignment, protected-subband scheduling, or admission;
- claiming hardware deployability of `q=0`-envelope stream redistribution
  without PA/RF-chain, latency, and signalling validation.

The deployable selected action is limited to serving sectors, at most two
external interferers per violated user, and at most two EESS contributors.
Global action solves are diagnostic oracles, not a distributed controller.
