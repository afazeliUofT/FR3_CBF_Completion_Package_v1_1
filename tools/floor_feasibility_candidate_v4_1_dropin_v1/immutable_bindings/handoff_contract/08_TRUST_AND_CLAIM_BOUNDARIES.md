
# Trust, Source, and Claim Boundaries

## Source hierarchy

1. Current GitHub head and immutable hashes.
2. Packaged latest diagnostic CSV/JSON.
3. Raw validated topology and review ZIPs.
4. Packaged milestone audits and summaries.
5. Terminal transcripts.
6. Scientific inference clearly labelled as inference.

## Facts established

- the Rorqual H100 environment and channel generation worked;
- incumbent safety passed for predictive across all five diagnostic passes;
- the service-floor failure is material;
- two physical users account for all current predictive floor violations;
- the full campaign has not been run;
- the 30 confirmatory seeds remain untouched by this diagnostic.

## Facts not established

- hardware-calibrated 65 dB null depth;
- statistical generalization over channel seeds;
- ephemeris uncertainty distribution;
- regulatory compliance;
- measured 100 ms fallback latency;
- final TWC acceptance readiness;
- feasibility of the current floor under an expanded action space.

## Scientific language rules

Use:

- “declared engineering envelope”;
- “excluded deployment smoke”;
- “one-seed/five-pass diagnostic”;
- “current evidence indicates”;
- “requires confirmatory campaign.”

Do not use:

- “proven practical compliance”;
- “calibrated uncertainty”;
- “field deployment ready”;
- “TWC result” for the current smoke;
- “the floor failure is numerical.”
