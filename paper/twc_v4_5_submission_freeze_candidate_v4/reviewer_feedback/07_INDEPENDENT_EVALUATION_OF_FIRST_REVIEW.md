# Independent Scientific Evaluation of the Reviewer Feedback

## Overall assessment

The review is technically strong and its central verdict is accepted: the work is a strong TWC candidate after a major revision whose mandatory content is provenance, scope, and internal consistency rather than a correction to the controller or the statistics. The review correctly identifies the paper's main contribution, the strength of the frozen fresh holdout, the validity of the feasibility-conditioned floor claim, and the distinction between action-class infeasibility and physical infeasibility.

The review is not accepted verbatim. Several statements are stronger than the available evidence and are narrowed below. No further algorithm variant or simulation campaign is justified by this feedback.

## Item-by-item disposition

### 1. SA.1027 threshold provenance — accepted with qualification

**Accepted:** Recommendation ITU-R SA.1027-6, Table 1, gives the terrestrial-path criteria for 8 025–8 400 MHz as −150 dBW per 10 MHz at 20% and −133 dBW per 10 MHz at 0.005%. The Recommendation states that both criteria must be met and that the minimum elevation is 5 degrees for this row. The archived pass begins at 5.012017 degrees.

**Qualified:** SA.1027 calls its table values single-entry sharing criteria. The aggregate power of the 57-sector cellular system is tested against one table entry as a conservative engineering convention. SA.1023 provides apportionment methodology, but it does not normatively declare a coordinated 57-sector network to be one physical interferer. The revised manuscript states this distinction and makes no regulatory-compliance claim.

**Disposition:** mandatory wording/citation repair; no rerun.

### 2. SA.509 scope and single-entry sensitivity — accepted with two corrections

**Accepted:** SA.509-3 is titled for space-research earth stations and radio astronomy, not EESS specifically. Its reference patterns are intended for large parabolic antennas with D/lambda at least 100 when measured data are unavailable. The frozen assumed dish has D = 13 m, G0 = 59.04 dBi, and D/lambda = 353.41, so the geometric applicability condition is satisfied. The source record confirms that a measured station pattern is unavailable. A single-entry sensitivity is therefore important.

**Correction 1:** the reviewer calls the single-entry curve a “certified bound” for a hard deterministic test. That is too strong. SA.509 supplies a reference peak envelope for the services named in the Recommendation. Without a measured EESS station pattern or an EESS-specific mandatory antenna standard, it is a conservative stress curve, not a certified real-station bound.

**Correction 2:** RA.769 precedent is unnecessary to the present justification and is not used in the revised manuscript.

**New exact sensitivity:** throughout the protected window, all off-axis angles are above the transition region and the single-entry expression is exactly 3 dB above the multiple-entry expression. Re-evaluating the frozen holdout actions by doubling their EESS ratios produces 83,859 long-criterion violation-seconds across all 150 pass cells and zero short-criterion violation-seconds. This establishes that the headline zero-violation result is conditional on the aggregate multiple-entry proxy. It does not establish what a reoptimized controller would achieve under the peak curve.

**Disposition:** mandatory scope and sensitivity repair; no new seed or controller run.

### 3. Array-description inconsistency — accepted; metadata-only repair

The inconsistency is real: one CSV declared 16x16 while the executed configuration and dual-polarization port-order audit specify an 8x8 dual-polarized panel with 64 element locations and 128 ports.

The executed generator constructs the array from the configuration, not from the inconsistent CSV array-size fields. The primary EESS coupling uses the element-pattern reference rows and does not consume the coherent-array-envelope column affected by the stale 16x16 label. The patched files correct the metadata and unused static sensitivity rows while leaving every primary scientific field unchanged.

**Disposition:** mandatory metadata repair; no scientific rerun.

### 4. TR 38.901 V19.4 gate — accepted through explicit scoping

The reviewer correctly notes that the package did not certify every used UMa pathloss, O2I, and LSP clause against TR 38.901 V19.4.0. The review itself offers two alternatives: complete such a certification or scope the paper to the exact software revision used.

The second alternative is adopted. The paper now states that the channels were generated by the pinned Sionna 2.0.1 UMa implementation, documented by Sionna as based on TR 38.901, at 8.15 GHz. It does not claim independent clause-by-clause V19.4.0 certification, a 3GPP calibration campaign, or universal channel-model validation.

**Disposition:** mandatory claim-scope repair; no post-hoc channel patch or rerun.

### 5. P.452 implementation provenance — accepted

The implementation was already validated against all 17 official ITU-R SG3 P.452-18 validation examples with maximum deviation below 1e-6 dB. The all-site audit covers 19 sites and 798 rows. The path profile uses NRCan MRDEM terrain. Terrain heights are also used as representative clutter heights (`g=h`), and no separate P.2108 term is added, avoiding double counting. The evaluated time percentages are direct annual percentages for an average year; no worst-month conversion is applied.

**Disposition:** mandatory manuscript provenance sentence; no rerun.

### 6. Fixed-service scope — accepted

SRSP-307.7 establishes Canadian fixed point-to-point use in 7 725–8 275 MHz and is relevant band-occupancy context. The present protected receiver and safety constraints concern only the modeled EESS earth station. Fixed-service receivers are outside the protected-service set and are not claimed to be protected.

**Disposition:** mandatory scope sentence.

### 7. Utility-effect interpretation — accepted with mathematical qualification

The final moving-PF effect of 0.2504677 over 228 users corresponds to an approximately 0.1099% increase in the **epsilon-shifted** geometric mean of final moving rates. It is not exactly a raw-rate geometric-mean increase because the objective is the sum of `log(Rbar + epsilon)`. The raw aggregate-capacity effect is modest; the main result is the combination of statistically significant PF improvement, zero simulated EESS violations under the declared model, and more than 80% floor-burden reduction.

**Disposition:** mandatory interpretation sentence with the epsilon qualification.

## Reviewer statements rejected or narrowed

1. **“Pre-registered holdout.”** Replaced by “Git-frozen and pre-specified before holdout execution.” No external preregistration registry is documented.
2. **“Network-as-single-entry reading is justified by SA.1023 n=1.”** Recast as an explicit conservative modeling convention with an algebraic n=1 analogy, not a normative classification.
3. **“The SA.509 peak envelope is the certified bound.”** Recast as a conservative reference-pattern stress; no measured actual-station pattern or EESS-specific mandatory pattern is available.
4. **“Real Gatineau/Cantley station.”** Recast as a public Gatineau reference geometry. The paper does not assert that all modeled antenna or operating parameters belong to an identified operational facility.
5. **“No manuscript claim is overstated.”** The original claim discipline was strong, but the provenance surface and SA.509 pattern dependence did require explicit narrowing. The revised manuscript now makes those boundaries visible.

## Optional recommendations

The review correctly labels wraparound, larger-array/frequency-grid sensitivity, an additional clairvoyant or beam-space reference, reassociation/multi-connectivity, and iid-pass-population sampling as optional. They are not submission blockers and are intentionally not pursued in this revision.

## Final verdict after revision

The seven mandatory issues are closed by citation, explicit scope, a metadata correction, and one exact archived-data sensitivity. The controller, frozen candidate, development results, and fresh-holdout statistics are unchanged. The revised paper is a strong TWC candidate, subject to coauthor reading and final editorial/submission checks.
